- Compare models on the TEST split.
- LightGBM and the neural models pick their stopping point on the validation split, so their validation scores are slightly optimistic.
- P@3 (regression): share of the 3 highest-forecast modules that had >= 1 incident. A fixed ranking of the busiest modules already scores well here; read it against historical_mean.
- P@3 (classification): share of the 3 highest-scored modules with >= 1 P1/P2.
- R@3: share of all P1/P2 module-days among that day's 3 flagged modules (recall under a 3-alerts-per-day budget; no threshold needed).
- recall/precision_at_threshold (JSON only) use the F1-optimal threshold chosen on validation.
